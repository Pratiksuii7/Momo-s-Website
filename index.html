Popular Types of Momo

Steamed Momo

It is a traditional steamed dumplings filled with minced meat or vegetables, served with a spicy dipping sauce.It is soft, juicy.It is much more delicious if we eat it with the sauce.

Fried Momo

It is crispy fried dumplings that offer a delightful crunch, filled with a savory mixture of meat or veggies. Golden brown perfection with a satisfying crispy exterior and tender filling. This is my favourite one because of is crispiness and flavor. You should try it once in a lifetime.

Momo

It is dumplings served in a flavorful spicy broth, perfect for a comforting meal on a cold day. This soup-style momo is a Nepali favorite that warms your heart and soul. It is best for someone who loves soup/Jhol. It is called jhol momo in nepali.

History of Momo

Origins in Tibet

Momo has many historical theory about how is it invented but the most popular and widely accepted one is that it was originated in tibet. The tibetean word "momo" known as "mog mog" that means "steamed bread". Tradittionaly, it was invented in tibet because of its cold temperature. At first, it was mainly made with vegetables and going through time it was starting to be made with yak's meat and later on many more varieties of momo were invented like chicken momo, fried momo, jhol/soup momo.

Introduction to Nepal

Momo was introduced to Nepal through Tibetan merchants and traders in kathmandu valley. Over time, it gained insane popularity and became an part of nepali food culture. Momo was evolved with local flavors and ingredients. Some Nepalese peoples loves momo more than any other food. It is now considered as a national food of Nepal.

Momo spreading in india

Momo made its way to India, particularly in the northeastern states, due to the influence of Tibetan culture and Nepalese migration. It quickly became a popular street food, with sellers offering various types of momo, including steamed, fried, and jhol momo. The Indian adaptation of momo often includes local spices and ingredients, making it a unique fusion dish. Today, momo is enjoyed by people across India and has become a beloved snack in many cities.

Simple Momo Recipe to Try at Home

Making momo at home can be a fun and rewarding experience. Here's a simple recipe to get you started:


Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 lb ground meat (chicken, pork, or beef) or finely chopped vegetables
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour and water to form a smooth dough. Cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. In another bowl, combine the ground meat or vegetables with onion, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roll out the dough into thin circles (about 3 inches in diameter).
  4. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each circle.Fold and pleat the edges to seal the momo.
  5. Steam the momos in a steamer for about 15-20 minutes until its cooked through.
  6. Serve hot with spicy sauce.

Enjoy your homemade momo!
